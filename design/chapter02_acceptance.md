## Density decision

The chapter contains 31 visible code cells after removing low-yield displays.
That is too much for one uninterrupted 90-minute meeting. Splitting the
material into another numbered chapter would work against the requested
14–15-chapter course and would separate numerical meaning from the data
contracts that give it scientific force. The accepted design therefore keeps
one navigation chapter with two explicit lesson movements and a natural pause
between Sections 2.9 and 2.10.

This is a deliberate pacing decision, not a waiver of the density concern.
The Chapter 1→2→3 neighbor audit must verify that Chapter 3 does not repeat
abundance, schema, Numba, Torch, or population-representation teaching. The
whole-book pacing audit may introduce a second web route under the same
Chapter 2 navigation item if the final reader still feels too long.
